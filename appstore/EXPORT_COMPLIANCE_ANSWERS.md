# Export Compliance (App Store Connect) - Draft Answers

Use these during build submission in App Store Connect if prompted.

## Encryption Questions
- Does your app use encryption? Yes
  - Reason: Uses standard Apple platform/network cryptography (HTTPS/TLS) only.
- Is your app designed to use proprietary or non-standard cryptography? No
- Is your app using exempt encryption only (standard OS crypto)? Yes

## Typical Result
- Most utility apps like this qualify for exemption and do not require separate export documentation.

If Apple shows different follow-up questions in your account, answer consistently with:
- no custom encryption algorithms
- no VPN/secure messaging/crypto-wallet functionality

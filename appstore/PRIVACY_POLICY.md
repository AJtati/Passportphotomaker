# Privacy Policy for Passport Photo Utility

Last updated: February 15, 2026

Passport Photo Utility ("the app") is a photo editing and print utility. This policy explains how information is handled.

## Summary
- We do not require account creation.
- We do not sell personal data.
- We do not run advertising SDKs in the app.
- We do not collect personal data for tracking.

## Information Handling
The app processes images and files you choose (such as photos and PDFs) to provide editing, resizing, compression, and export features.

Processing happens on-device for core features.

## Permissions
The app may request:
- Camera access: to capture a photo for editing.
- Photo Library access: to import photos.
- Photo Library Add access: to save generated output.

You can deny these permissions, but related features may not work.

## Data Sharing
The app does not intentionally share your personal data with third parties for advertising or tracking.

## Data Retention
Generated files are stored according to your actions (for example, saving to your device or sharing). You control removal from your device storage.

## Children’s Privacy
The app is not directed specifically to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Security
Reasonable technical measures are used within the app workflow. No method is perfectly secure.

## Changes to This Policy
This policy may be updated over time. The "Last updated" date reflects the latest version.

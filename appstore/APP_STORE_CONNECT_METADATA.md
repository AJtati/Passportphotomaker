## Description
Passport Photo Utility helps you prepare print-ready photos directly on your device.

Features:
- Passport photo creator with crop tools and fixed aspect ratio support
- Preset and custom photo dimensions in mm/inch
- Auto layout on paper sizes for print-ready output
- Multi-photo print sheet generation
- Collage print editor with move, resize, and rotate controls
- Image resizing and compression tools
- PDF compression and export options
- Export as JPG, PNG, or PDF
- Share or save generated files from iPhone/iPad

The app is designed for practical photo prep workflows such as passport photos, visa photos, and general print-size photo formatting.

No account is required to use core features.
